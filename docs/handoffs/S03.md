## Review findings resolved

An independent read-only reviewer reproduced three concrete issues and reran the same scenarios after their fixes:

1. DEMO at an already-correct note snapshot could leave a pending grade active. DEMO now always invalidates it, enters editing, and requests audio cancellation; an old result cannot bypass the return/keep choice.
2. Object-based restore could retain nested evidence references owned by the caller. Restored evidence is detached, so later caller mutations cannot change the accepted snapshot.
3. A forged known successful first submission in an unrepaired editing phase could pass validation and appear independent. Restore now validates cross-field repair/first-submission consistency, and independent repair also requires final success.

These fixes have checked-in regression tests. The reviewer confirmed all three resolved and reported no remaining high-impact blocker.
